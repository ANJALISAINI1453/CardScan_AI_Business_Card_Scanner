CardScan AI - Business Card Scanner

1. Copy .env.example to .env and enter your Gemini API key.
2. Put your Google service-account JSON at credentials/service_account.json.
3. Complete GOOGLE_SHEETS_SETUP.txt once.
4. Double-click RUN.bat.
5. Open http://127.0.0.1:5000
6. Scan -> review/edit -> Save to Google Sheet.

Every saved card is appended as a new row in ONE Google Sheet. Local Excel save/download remains available too.
